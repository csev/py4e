Relational Databases: JOIN for Data Reconstruction

relational_database, join, foreign_key, primary_key, on_clause

Relational databases use the JOIN operation to combine data from multiple tables. The ON clause specifies matching conditions between foreign keys and primary keys to include only compatible rows, preventing the inefficient Cartesian product that occurs without it.

This technique reconstructs user-visible data by joining tables such as track, genre, album, and artist. The database stores only relationship structures without vertical replication, and the join operation dynamically produces the required vertical data structures for the user interface.
