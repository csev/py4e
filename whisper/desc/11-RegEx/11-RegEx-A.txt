Python Regular Expressions: Pattern Matching

python, regex, pattern, matching, special_chars

Regular expressions are a character-based language for pattern matching and text extraction. They are distributed with Python and require importing the re module to use functions such as re.search and re.findall.

Special characters in regular expressions include the caret (^) for the start of a line, the dot (.) as a wildcard for any character, the asterisk (*) for zero or more repetitions, and the plus (+) for one or more repetitions. These characters modify the behavior of the immediately preceding character.
